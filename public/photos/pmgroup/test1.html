About Our Group

Welcome to my personal group webpage. Myself, Dr. Padmabati Mondal, is working as an assistant professor in the Department of Chemistry at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER) Tirupati, India. My research group is interested in excited state quantum chemistry including photochemistry and spectroscopy for transition metal complexes and organic chromophores and multi-scale modeling and simulations for biomolecules.

Our group combines theoretical and computational approaches to understand electronic structure, photophysical properties, and excited-state dynamics in complex molecular systems. We develop and apply quantum chemical methods and multi-scale modeling techniques to solve problems at the interface of chemistry, physics, and biology.

Research Areas

Excited State Quantum Chemistry

Development and application of computational methods to model excited electronic states and photochemical processes in complex molecular systems.

Transition Metal Complexes

Investigating the electronic structure and photophysical properties of transition metal complexes for applications in photocatalysis, sensing, and energy conversion.

Organic Chromophores

Exploring the structure-property relationships in organic chromophores for applications in optical materials, bioimaging, and phototherapy.

Multi-scale Modeling for Biomolecules

Developing and applying multi-scale modeling approaches to study structure, dynamics, and function of biomolecular systems across multiple time and length scales.
